## The three layers, and why they stack instead of cancelling

Cashback is an affiliate mechanic. When you click from ShopBack to a store and buy, the store's affiliate system reports the sale as coming from ShopBack. The store pays ShopBack a commission. ShopBack passes a chunk of that commission back to you. That's the whole business model, and it's also why a store can't attribute your purchase if something interferes with the click — no attribution, no commission, no cashback.

Here's the part most cashback explanations skip. A single purchase can earn on three separate layers at once:

- Cashback, funded by the merchant's affiliate budget
- A promo code, funded by the merchant's marketing budget
- Card rewards or points, funded by your card issuer's economics

None of them draws on the same dollar, so none of them cancels another. A $200 order at 8% cashback, with a 10% merchant code, on a card giving 2% back, stacks. Cashback isn't a discount you're choosing instead of the promo code.

## Four steps between buying and getting paid

The flow is short, and every step has a failure mode.

1. **Start at ShopBack.** Find the store in the app or site and click through. That click drops a tracking cookie that ties the purchase to your account. The Chrome, Edge and Firefox extension exists purely to automate this — it detects partner stores and offers one-click activation, so you don't have to remember to begin at ShopBack.
2. **Buy in one uninterrupted session.** Same device, same browser, start to finish.
3. **Wait up to 48 hours for the order to appear as Pending.** That's tracking, not approval.
4. **Wait for Confirmed.** The store validates the order against its own cashback rules, then runs a cooling period to make sure you don't return or cancel it. Only then does the money become withdrawable.

Your account shows three statuses: Pending, Confirmed, Rejected. Pending is the one that generates support tickets, and it's usually not a problem — it means the store is still processing. Bonus cashback works the same way; if you've met the store's requirements, it should confirm within about five working days.

Travel is the outlier worth knowing about. Hotel and flight cashback typically validates after your stay or trip is completed, not at booking. So a booking you make in March might not turn into withdrawable money until May. That's normal, not a red flag.

## Where cashback gets voided

This is the section that decides whether the platform works for you. Most "cashback didn't track" cases are one of these:

- **Ad blockers and privacy extensions.** They block the tracking cookie. Allowlist ShopBack and the store, or turn them off for the session.
- **Leaving the tab.** Googling the product, comparing prices on another site, clicking a banner ad or visiting a coupon site between activation and checkout can overwrite the attribution. That last one is the most common cause of rejected cashback.
- **Coupon codes you found elsewhere.** Only use codes listed in the store's cashback terms on ShopBack. An unlisted code can void the whole purchase.
- **Switching devices mid-purchase.** Start and finish in the same browser or app. Starting on the ShopBack website and finishing in the ShopBack app is explicitly called out as a way to lose the tracking.
- **Shopping a foreign version of the store.** Buying from a Korean storefront while your account is Singapore-based will most likely get rejected.
- **Assumptions about what counts.** Most stores exclude shipping and taxes from the cashback calculation, and many exclude gift cards or specific product categories.
- **Returns and cancellations.** Obvious, but worth stating: rejected orders mean rejected cashback.

For in-store earning, the misses are different. Card-linked offers only match the specific card you enrolled, so paying with a different card kills it, and forgetting to activate the offer first kills it too.

One in-store mechanic looks like a bug but isn't. In some markets, ShopBack Pay uses a "return cashback" model: your first purchase at a store shows as Pending and only confirms once you make a second transaction there within the required timeframe. If you're a one-time visitor, that pending amount stays pending.

## Getting the payout right

Payout details differ by market. For ShopBack US, confirmed cashback can be withdrawn once it reaches $10, to PayPal or by ACH transfer, with a daily withdrawal limit of up to $300. Processing typically takes about 10 calendar days and can run to 30 if batches or bank checks slow down.

Two practical notes. You can't apply available cashback as a discount on an online order at checkout — it's cash out, or you convert it into ShopBack gift cards for partner stores, or you spend it in person through ShopBack Pay where that's supported. And if your balance is above the minimum, withdrawing before a Plus renewal date keeps the fee from being deducted from cashback you'd rather hold.

If something genuinely doesn't track, file a missing cashback claim in the app within the stated window. Include the merchant, order number and date, order subtotal before tax and shipping, the device and browser you used, whether you applied a coupon and where you found it, and a screenshot of your confirmation. Claims with all of that get resolved faster than claims with the order number alone.
